# Surface Brief: MediChain AI Patient Dashboard

## Scope & Visitor Mode
- **Scope:** Patient primary workspace & health intelligence dashboard (`/`)
- **Visitor Mode:** Operate (task-driven clinical tool; the tool disappears into the task)
- **Primary Audience:** Asha Sharma, 44, managing Type-2 diabetes with quarterly lab panels
- **Core Jobs:**
  1. Check glycemic trajectory at a glance without clinical anxiety
  2. Drop and securely store a new lab report
  3. Verify authentic origin and manage doctor sharing permissions with 1-tap revocation

## Direction contract

### THESIS
The dashboard is a serene, human clinical instrument that replaces developer plumbing and KPI confetti with one unmistakable health trajectory and one clear action. It refuses card-in-card nesting, tracked uppercase eyebrows, raw blockchain hashes on screen, and fragmented portal navigation.

### OWN-WORLD
Warm morning paper ground (`#F6F4EE`) under soft daylight, crisp white single-layer sheets with hairline boundaries (`#E5E7EB`), deep clinical ink (`#111827`), and a single authoritative trust accent (`#1F7A6D`) applied to under 5% of the viewport.

### STORY
Asha opens the dashboard in the morning. She is greeted with reassurance: her HbA1c is down to 7.4%. She drops her newest lab report, sees it instantly verified with a quiet green seal, and confirms Dr. Rao has active access, with the peace of mind that she can revoke it with a single tap.

### FIRST VIEWPORT
A serene masthead (MediChain AI, Asha Sharma, subtle mode tabs: Overview, Reports, Doctor Access). Immediately beneath, an empathetic greeting (*"Good morning, Asha. Your blood sugar is heading in the right direction."*) leading into a quiet HbA1c curve showing the 3-panel descent from 8.6% to 7.4%. Directly below sits the primary action: a tactile, inviting drag-and-drop zone (*"Drop a new lab report here to add to your records"*).
